## 🔐 Face ID-Style Unlocking

**Look at your camera. Let LunarNotch recognize you.**

Powered by [Glance](https://www.tryglance.app), LunarNotch introduces a Face ID-inspired experience for your Mac.

Simply look toward your camera and experience a convenient, futuristic unlocking interaction.

> [!CAUTION]
> **This is an experimental convenience feature, NOT a security mechanism.**
>
> - It is not equivalent to Apple's Face ID or Touch ID.
> - A photograph or other spoofing method may bypass recognition.
> - Do not rely on it to protect sensitive information.
> - Neither LunarNotch nor Glance guarantees protection against unauthorized access through this feature.
